About the Firm

A general practice, built to stay with a matter to its end.

Babar & Babar was founded on a simple premise: clients don't need a specialist for every narrow field — they need one resolute advocate who can competently carry a family matter, a property dispute, or a legal notice through to resolution, and who actually answers the phone between hearings.

Principal Advocate

Babar Ali

Babar Ali is the founding and principal advocate of the firm, practising before the Civil Court,Sessions Court,High Court and Supreme Court of Pakistan. His practice spans family law, civil litigation, property matters, and corporate and tax advisory work for established business firms.

He built the firm around a straightforward standard: a client should never have to chase their own lawyer for an update. Every open file is reviewed ahead of its next hearing date, and clients are briefed in plain language rather than legal shorthand.

How We Work

Three commitments, kept on every file.

  • 01

    Plain-language advice

    Every client receives a clear summary of their position and options in everyday language — never just a rushed opinion at the counter.

  • 02

    Honest cost estimates

    Fees are discussed and agreed before any filing is made. No matter proceeds on an open-ended understanding of cost.

  • 03

    A single point of contact

    Your matter is not handed between juniors. The advocate who takes your first consultation stays on your file.
