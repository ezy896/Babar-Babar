Legal Services

Areas of Practice

Comprehensive trial and appellate advocacy, revenue court representation, and advisory services tailored for clients in Punjab.

Court Advocacy

Civil Litigation & Appeals

⚖

We represent clients in trial courts across Pakistan and in appellate matters before the Lahore High Court and the Supreme Court of Pakistan. Our civil litigation practice encompasses declaration suits, recovery of money/dues, permanent and temporary injunctions, execution proceedings, and specific performance of contracts.

Key Services:

  • • Suits for Declaration & Permanent Injunction
  • • Recovery Suits & Summary Procedure
  • • Execution Petitions & Decree Enforcement
  • • First and Second Appeals before District & High Court

Land & Real Estate

Property Law & Revenue Matters

🏠

Land matters require sharp understanding of both revenue records (Fard, Jamabandi, Aks Shajra) and civil procedure. We assist clients with partition of agricultural and urban land, illegal dispossession remedies, challenging fraudulent transfers, and revenue court litigation before Tehsildars, Assistant Commissioners, and Revenue Boards.

Key Services:

  • • Land Partition (Taqseem) Suits
  • • Mutation (Inteqal) Disputes & Record Correction
  • • Cancellation of Fraudulent Sale Deeds
  • • Illegal Dispossession Act Petitions

Family Disputes

Family Law & Child Custody

👪

Family disputes are sensitive and time-critical. We guide clients with utmost discretion and determination in cases relating to dissolution of marriage, Khula, recovery of dowry articles, maintenance for wives and minor children, and child custody / visitation schedules before Family Courts and Guardian Courts.

Key Services:

  • • Khula & Dissolution of Marriage
  • • Dowry Articles (Jahez) & Dower (Mehr) Recovery
  • • Maintenance for Wife & Children (Kharcha)
  • • Guardian Petitions & Minor Custody Matters

Commercial Advisory

Corporate, Business & Tax Law

💼

For regional enterprises, traders, suppliers, and agricultural businesses, we provide corporate contract drafting, partnership deeds, dispute negotiation, recovery notices, and advisory regarding FBR and PRA tax notices.

Key Services:

  • • Partnership Deeds & Business Registration
  • • Commercial Contracts & Vendor Agreements
  • • FBR & PRA Notices & Tax Compliance
  • • Labour & Employment Dispute Settlements
